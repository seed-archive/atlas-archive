# Nutrient Processor recipes

**What can you cook in the Nutrient Processor?** Pick up to three ingredients, or the dish you want, and the table keeps every recipe that fits; most dishes can be cooked more than one way, and each way is a row.

<!-- widget:book -->

Tap a dish for its card. Besides what it sells for, the card gives the bonus the dish grants in the Nutrient Ingestor, one of the [exosuit modules](/guides/exosuit-modules), and how long one unit lasts; eaten by hand, a dish gives only a short effect of its own. Raw catches cook too: [fishing](/guides/fishing) has every fish, and the crops of a farm are on [farming](/guides/farming). Which dishes sell best and which protect you best are on [best-selling dishes](/guides/best-selling-dishes) and [food for hazards](/guides/food-for-hazards). What a refiner makes is on [refiner recipes](/tools/refiner-recipes).
