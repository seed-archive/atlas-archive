# Refiner recipes

**What can a refiner make?** Pick what you put in, slot by slot, or what you want out, and the table keeps every recipe that fits. One slot is the Portable Refiner, two the Medium, three the Large, and a recipe that fits a smaller refiner works in every larger one.

<!-- widget:book -->

Which recipes loop back with more than went in is on [refiner loops](/guides/refiner-loops), and which pay or make nanites on [refining for money](/guides/refining-for-money). You need not build a refiner: the Personal Refiner, one of the [exosuit modules](/guides/exosuit-modules), is a Portable Refiner inside your exosuit, and the Personal Refiner Mk 2 a Medium one.

Tap any item for its card: what it sells for, what makes it, and what it goes into. Dishes come out of the Nutrient Processor, on [Nutrient Processor recipes](/tools/cooking-recipes), and parts you build by hand are on [crafting recipes](/tools/crafting-recipes).
