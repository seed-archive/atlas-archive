# Starships

**What shapes can a starship have?** This page puts an exotic, a solar ship, a Fighter, a hauler, an explorer, or a shuttle together from its parts: pick the type, then the parts. Every shape here is one the game makes. Searching for a system is not built yet.

## How to use it

Click a part of the ship, or a name under it, to see its choices; each tile draws its part. Every pick is a ship the game builds, so no choice is greyed out. The ship is drawn in plain grey; an exotic stands as it lands, with its wings raised.

An exotic ship has one of two bodies. The Royal body carries an engine, a pair of wings, and a top wing, each one of three shapes; one engine takes a second set of thrusters, and two of the top wings carry one of five details. The squid is the rare body: about one exotic in 21, always the same shape.

A solar ship has four parts, each one of a few shapes: the wing tips, the wings, the body, and the sails, drawn spread open.

A Fighter has an engine, wings, and a cockpit. Most wings come with or without an accessory, and several carry sub-wings of their own; one cockpit takes one of five noses.

A hauler has a cockpit and a hull. The hull carries wings, each with thrusters and sub-wings, side containers, and engines; some hulls go without wings.

An explorer has one of two cockpits, each with its canopy and accessories, and wings that are either a matched pair or a left and a right wing of their own; a wing can carry a tip, sub-wings, or an accessory.

A shuttle is single or double. Each kind carries its own cockpits, hulls, wings, thrusters, lamps, and accessories, so the choices change with the body you pick.
