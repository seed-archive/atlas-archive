# Best creatures

**Which creature has the best stats?** One graded S, S, S whose numbers reach the ceiling: {{healthCeiling}} Health, {{agilityCeiling}} Agility, or {{combatCeiling}} Combat Effectiveness. The best found stop one short, at {{healthBest}}, {{agilityBest}}, and {{combatBest}}.

## Three ceilings

A letter is a band, and the creature's number sits somewhere inside it ([creature stats](/guides/creature-stats)). Health and Agility pull apart with size: Health is highest on the largest individual of a species, Agility on the smallest, while Combat Effectiveness does not care. So each stat stands as its own nomination, read where it peaks. Only {{allS}} of the creatures you can adopt are graded S, S, S at all.
