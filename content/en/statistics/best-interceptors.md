# Best Interceptors

**What is the best Sentinel Interceptor?** A class S wreck with supercharged slots in a square and all four base stats at the top of their range. The best one found reaches {{lowest}}% of the range on its weakest stat; none sits at the top on all four.

## How close the best come

A [Sentinel Interceptor](/guides/interceptors) rolls damage, shields, hyperdrive, and maneuverability, each inside the range of its class. The table reads them as the ship holds them once claimed, for wrecks whose supercharged slots form a [square](/guides/ship-square), and shows each stat as a share of its range. Where a ship you claimed stands among them, [How lucky am I?](/tools/how-lucky-am-i) scores from 0 to 100.

The best one found is {{best}}: its four stats stand at {{everyStat}} percent of their ranges.

## How often a wreck gets there

Four stats make the top the hardest of any item: a roll lands on the very top about once in {{oneTop}}, and the best wreck needs that on all four. Even close is rare: {{near}} of class S Interceptors with a square have every stat at 90% of the range or more, and at 95% or more the share is {{at95}}. So a wreck with every stat above nine tenths is already one to keep: waiting for a better one means waiting for a draw rarer than any found so far.

Stats do not set what a wreck sells for; its cargo slots do, as [Interceptor prices](/guides/interceptor-prices) shows.
