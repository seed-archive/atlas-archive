# Best multitools

**What is the best class S multitool?** One with supercharged slots in a square and every base stat at the very top of its range. The best rifle comes within reach of it, {{rifleLowest}}% of the range on its weaker stat, yet no multitool sits at the top on every stat.

## How close the best come

A class S multitool rolls each of its base stats somewhere inside a range, the one on [multitool stats](/guides/multitool-stats). The table takes only multitools whose supercharged slots form a [square](/guides/square), since that is the shape the strongest build needs, and shows the best of each type: its weakest stat and every stat, as a share of the range.

<!-- widget:tools -->

A terminal decides the stats of everything it offers at once. Every multitool a shop or a monolith shows, whatever its type, rolls from the same draw, so the best plain and the best yellow Atlantid are two offers of one terminal, and so are the best Sentinel pistol and rifle. One terminal near the top is worth checking for every type it sells.

## How often a find gets there

A roll lands on the very top of its range about once in {{oneTop}}, and the best multitool needs that on every stat at once. Coming close is far easier: {{rifleNear}} of class S rifles with a square have every stat at 99.9% of the range or more.
