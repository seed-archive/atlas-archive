# Deep-Space Outpost

**What does a Deep-Space Outpost sell?** Charts to deep space, the salvage of hulks, and contraband. Its merchant shows {{shownMin}} items from a list of {{wares}}: the Habitable Asteroid Chart and the Salvage Claim always, the rest drawn.

## What it sells

Every item it can show, with the description the game gives it and its value. A Habitable Asteroid Chart marks an asteroid where a base can be built; a Salvage Claim marks a large wreck rich with salvage. Every other chart, and where it comes from, is on [charts](/guides/charts).

## The Salvage Processor

Grab salvage in space with the Gravitino Coil and throw it into the outpost's Salvage Processor: each object turns into one item in your inventory. These are the materials of [space station decoration](/guides/station-decoration), and many sell well.

## Courier contracts

The outpost's mission board offers courier contracts: take a parcel from its Delivery Management terminal with the Gravitino Coil, stow it aboard your corvette, and fly it to a postbox or a space station of the same system. Without a corvette and the Gravitino Coil the board offers none. They make up {{courierShare}} of what the board offers, and {{courierShareDone}} once you have cleared the system's hulks, infested outposts, and waste sites, whose own missions then drop off the board. A contract can be abandoned at no cost.

At the edges of a deep-space outpost you can summon the [Forsaken Trader](/guides/forsaken-trader), who trades biomechanical corvette parts for Haunting Mementos. The hulks a Salvage Claim marks can hold jars of them.
