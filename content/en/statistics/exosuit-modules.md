# Exosuit modules

**What can an exosuit upgrade module raise?** Each system of the suit has its own family, and the class sets how many stats one install raises and how far: an S-Class Movement System upgrade always raises jetpack tanks and initial boost power, and two more of four other stats. The exosuit merchant of a station sells them, as [station technology merchants](/guides/technology-merchants) shows.

Each stat reads as the module's card prints it. A protection module's card shows no numbers, so its table lists only the classes. Illegal modules are sold at an [Outlaw Station](/guides/outlaw-station) and now and then by the [Scrap Dealer](/guides/scrap-dealer), Rusted ones are sea salvage, the Forbidden module is a Sentinel one, and the salvaged components are Autophage.

## Upgrade modules

One table per family: each class, how many stats one install raises, and the limits of each stat. Below each table a second one shows what one install rolls: how many stats it raises, how often it comes near the best its kind can add to the stat it names, and how often each stat is raised; a third shows how often a raised stat lands in the top tenth of its limits, where the Illegal, Forbidden, and salvaged modules stand out by a one-in-ten draw that raises the most stats their kind allows.

Under the three tables of a family stands what to take: how an A-Class or an Illegal install compares with an S-Class one in what the family is for, as often as it adds at least what a middling S-Class install adds, more than the best one, or less than the worst.

### Forbidden module

A Forbidden module is one module for the whole suit: an install raises some of six stats, {{sentsuitForbiddenLimits}}. How many it raises is a draw: {{sentsuitForbiddenStats}}. Its Life Support Tanks and Solar Panel Power can go past an S-Class Life Support module's, and its Shield Strength as high as an S-Class Defence Systems module's, so read the card of each one you get: the stats it happened to raise decide whether it earns a slot. {{sentsuitForbiddenNear}} of installs come near the most Shield Strength it can add.

### Salvaged components

The Autophage's salvaged components roll exactly as a Forbidden module does: the same six stats, the same limits, and the same draw of how many are raised.

## Supercharged slots

The exosuit has three supercharged slots, and they stand in the same places for every player: slot 5 of the top row of its technology inventory, slot 9 of the second row, slot 3 of the third row, counting from the left. There is nothing to roll and nothing to farm. None of the three is adjacent to another, so there is no figure to make: each can lift one system of the suit. Put one module of a kind on it, and set the other modules of that kind and the technology they upgrade around it, adjacent, as on the exocraft. Each placement adds as much on the suit as on a multitool, by the rule on [supercharged slots](/guides/supercharged-slots): the supercharged slot lifts the module on it, and each adjacent module of its kind, and the technology it upgrades, add a little more.

## Every exosuit module

The modules you build or receive, apart from upgrade modules. The [Base Computer Archives](/quests/base-computer-archives) of your base give the Shield Lattice and the Neural Stimulator, and the story, [The Artemis Path](/quests/the-artemis-path), gives Artemis' Translator and, at its end, Remembrance. [The Atlas Path](/quests/the-atlas-path) ends with the Star Seed or the Mark of the Denier, by the choice you make at its last interface. The Haz-Mat Gauntlet lets you harvest most [farm plants](/guides/farming). The Nutrient Ingestor eats the dishes you cook, one at a time, for a bonus: to scan rewards, as [food for scanning](/guides/food-for-scanning) shows, or to a hazard's drain, on [food for hazards](/guides/food-for-hazards).

## Jetpack trails

A jetpack trail colours the jet behind you. It is an Exosuit Appearance Override, not technology: it takes a slot of the exosuit and changes no stat, unlike a [starship trail](/guides/starship-modules), which adds a little boost. Most are sold for Quicksilver, a few are expedition rewards.
