# Forsaken Trader

**What does the Forsaken Trader sell?** Corvette parts of a biomechanical kind, {{wares}} of them, from cockpits and habs to thrusters, wings, and hull pieces, traded for Haunting Mementos you gather in space hulks, derelict freighters, infested outposts, and dig sites; and, for {{offeringAmount}} Preserved Grubs, the Larva Cannon, an Alien multitool of class A.

## How to summon one

Every inhabited system has a [Deep-Space Outpost](/guides/deep-space-outpost), and at its edge stands a Summoning Terminal that calls a Forsaken Trader. The first one you meet wants a memento before it opens its collection. Each has every one of its items on sale.

## Where to find Haunting Mementos

There are six mementos, the Preserved Brain, Grub, Sample, Eyeball, Egg, and Horror, each in a jar or a crate. Take a jar or a crate to get its memento: shoot it instead and it breaks into {{destroyedMin}} to {{destroyedMax}} nanites, with no memento. The Haunted Cache of a derelict freighter holds several mementos of one kind, and every derelict freighter has one, in the room of its last terminal.

An infested outpost holds the most. On a space hulk, take the jars first: pulling parts off can set off a meltdown that blows the hulk {{meltdownMin}} to {{meltdownMax}} minutes later, and a radioactive core sets it off at once. The hidden space hulks are what the Salvage Claim of the same outpost points to, and the place of a derelict freighter is sold by the [Scrap Dealer](/guides/scrap-dealer) of a space station. The {{titleMementos}}th memento you gather gives the title Mind Harvester and the Larva Tank helmet.

## Boundary Horrors

Standing near a jar or a Haunted Cache wears down your boundary, a bar of its own beside your hazard protection, and it does not come back by itself; step away and it stops falling. Opening a cache takes {{cacheDrain}} of its 100 at once, enough to call Boundary Horrors. Once it has weakened by {{firstHorror}}, a Boundary Horror comes; the weaker it gets, the more come at once, and from {{packFrom}} on they come in packs, on a planet and in space alike. Each has {{horrorHealth}} health. The Elixir of Glass restores {{elixirRestore}} of the boundary, but only The Cursed, the sixteenth of the [expeditions](/quests/expeditions), taught its recipe, as the [crafting recipes](/tools/crafting-recipes) show.

## What it sells

Every item it can show, with the description the game gives it and the value the trade counts for it. A few cockpits, thrusters, and reactors come from no one else, as [corvettes](/guides/corvettes) shows.

## What it takes

The trader takes the Haunting Mementos, each counted at {{wantedValue}}, and asks {{barter}} times the value of what you take: it sells below value. It rates each memento at {{floor}} to the whole of its value, and an offer has {{slots}} slots, each holding a stack, so the last column of the stock is how many mementos buy each part whatever the rating; fewer may still pass, with a smaller chance. The answer stays the same for the same offer until you next fly into space, so after a refusal fly out and back, or change the offer.

## The Larva Cannon

Outside its stock, the trader takes an offering for one item: choose Make Offering when you talk to it, and {{offeringAmount}} Preserved Grubs buy the Larva Cannon. The Larva Cannon is an Alien multitool of class A, with the same layout of slots and the same supercharged slots for every player, and comes with its mining beam, scanner, analysis visor, terrain manipulator, and Boltcaster installed, two upgrade modules of class X among them. Think before upgrading it to class S: the upgrade rolls its base stats again as a pistol's, which have no damage.

What the parts add to a corvette is in the [corvette modules](/guides/corvette-modules) guide. The [Salvage Exchange](/guides/salvage-exchange) of a space station trades corvette parts too, part for part.
