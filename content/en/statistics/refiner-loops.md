# Refiner loops

**Which refiner loop pays best?** {{top}}: round its loop it gives back {{topGain}} times the value that goes in, and {{topStart}} times the count, after paying for everything its steps take on top.

A loop starts and ends with the same item. Each step is a refiner recipe, and a step of two or three ingredients needs the others on top; a loop counts them as paid, at their base value, so only a loop that gives back more units than goes in stands here. Every drawing reads from the top down, as the game's assembly steps do: the item that comes out, then what made it, down to the start you put in. One circle of items is one card; the variants of a circle take other additives.

Every recipe of the three refiners is on [refiner recipes](/tools/refiner-recipes), and the Personal Refiner in your exosuit runs the ones of one ingredient. A refiner also turns cheap resources into dearer ones in a single step: [refining for money](/guides/refining-for-money).
