# Space station decoration

**What can you build around your space station, and what does it cost?** {{parts}} exterior decorations, every one of them different, built from the station's build menu on the rings around it; each costs the materials below, most of them salvage you bring to a [Deep-Space Outpost](/guides/deep-space-outpost).

## How you build one

Choose a decoration in the build menu of the station you direct, pick a point on one of the rings around the station, and the materials are taken from your inventory. A decoration is never an item you hold: only its materials are. The blueprints come at the Construction Research Station of the Space Anomaly for nanites; {{rare}} rare ones you learn from the objects they copy, found in deep space. No decoration gives a bonus: they are for the look of the station.
